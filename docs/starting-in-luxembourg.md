# Founded in Luxembourg, Built for Global & Cross-Border Families

Clarvia ASBL was founded in Luxembourg because cross-border administrative complexity is part of everyday life here: a single family situation frequently spans multiple countries, nationalities, languages, health and pension systems, civil registries, and legal traditions.

From that starting point, Clarvia has grown into a global public-interest service:

- **1,000-page multilingual guidance library** ([clarvia.org/en/guidance](https://clarvia.org/en/guidance)) covering **592 canonical guides** across all **196 countries** (`392` single-country terminal illness and bereavement guides) and **200 cross-border corridors** in **14 languages**.
- **Ask Clarvia** ([clarvia.org/en/ask](https://clarvia.org/en/ask)) providing free, source-cited email answers to specific terminal illness, bereavement, and cross-border administrative questions in 100+ languages.
- **Interactive bereavement checklist & open consequence graph** ([clarvia.org/en/checklist](https://clarvia.org/en/checklist)), where Luxembourg serves as the first deep reference implementation for our formal CPSV-AP / CCCEV / ELI / PROV-O consequence graph (`Clarvia Graph`) and normalized legislation corpus (`lex/`).

Being rooted in Luxembourg grounds our engineering in real multilingual, cross-border realities — while our published guidance library and Ask Clarvia serve families worldwide.
