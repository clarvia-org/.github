# Governance

## Organisation

Clarvia is maintained by **CLARVIA ASBL**, a Luxembourg non-profit association registered under RCS F15680. The association's statutory purpose is to support bereaved individuals and families through structured administrative guidance.

## Decision-making

### Data accuracy decisions

Workflow data — deadlines, prerequisites, legal references — must be verifiably correct. Changes to legal obligations require:

1. A cited primary source (statute, regulation, official government publication).
2. Review by at least one maintainer with domain knowledge of the relevant jurisdiction.
3. Approval before merge. No self-merging of data changes.

### Technical decisions

Architecture, tooling, and infrastructure decisions are made by the **maintainers** team through discussion on the relevant issue or pull request. Consensus is preferred; when consensus cannot be reached, the project lead decides.

### Roadmap decisions

Strategic direction, country expansion, and partnership decisions are made by the CLARVIA ASBL board.

## Teams

| Team | Role |
|---|---|
| **maintainers** | Full admin access. Responsible for releases, reviews, and project direction. |
| **reviewers** | Triage issues and review data contributions. |
| **data-editors** | Contribute and maintain workflow data (tasks, sources, translations). |
| **web-maintainers** | Maintain the web publishing layer. |
| **ops** | Manage operations, infrastructure, and non-public planning. |

## Becoming a contributor

1. Start by opening issues or reviewing existing ones.
2. Submit a pull request. Quality contributions over time lead to team membership invitations.
3. Team membership is granted by maintainers based on sustained, constructive participation.

## Amendments

This governance document may be amended by the maintainers team with board approval. Changes are tracked via pull request.
